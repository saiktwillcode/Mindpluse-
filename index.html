Why scores alone fail students

The problem

Feedback, journals and forum posts hold early signs of overload, but "stressed" and "not stressed anymore" look identical to keyword counters.

The approach

Clause-level reading with negation, intensity, hedging and contrast ("but") handling, then workload, engagement and trend analysis.

The result

An explained indicator, an early signal over time and practical next steps, never a diagnosis.
